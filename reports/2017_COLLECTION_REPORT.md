# SBI Clerk 2017 collection report

**Scope:** 2017 only; search, direct-source inspection, one recovery pass, and validation. No question-pattern or preparation analysis was performed.

## Result

No directly accessible document was established as an SBI Clerk 2017 Prelims or Mains paper. The two staged entries therefore remain `MISSING_SOURCE`; no questions were created or imported.

Two independent secondary archives explicitly state that SBI Clerk was not conducted in 2017. An apparent counterexample on Prepp was investigated through its direct linked documents: its SBI-labelled 2017 links lead to documents headed/named **IBPS Clerk** and, for one Mains link, **IBPS PO**. Those documents are excluded rather than misfiled as SBI Clerk.
