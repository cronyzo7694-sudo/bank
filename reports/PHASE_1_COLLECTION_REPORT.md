## Reading this inventory

A `PARTIAL` / `UNVERIFIED` record means a reputable-secondary archive page identifies a memory-based compilation, but its direct document has not been inspected and its question wording has **not** been copied into this repository. It is not represented as an official SBI paper or a complete question paper. An empty `questions` array means *not collected*, never “zero questions asked.”

`MISSING_SOURCE` marks a deliberate gap. It contains no source ID and no questions.

## Source caveat and next collection work

The source registry is at [`sources/source_registry.json`](../sources/source_registry.json). The evidence notes in each individual paper JSON preserve what the source actually identifies. Before a record can become `COMPLETE`, a collector must inspect the direct PDF/page, preserve every available original question number and wording, record options and source-backed answers (or `UNKNOWN`), preserve section labels, and cross-check an independent source where available. No missing text may be reconstructed.
